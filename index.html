Writing & Research

Published thinking on where AI, work, and education intersect.

Human vs. LLM Performance in Data Extraction

RESEARCH ARTICLE · RICE UNIVERSITY · DEC 2025

Benchmarks five leading large language models (Gemini 2.5 Pro, Grok, Claude Sonnet 4.5, ChatGPT, and DeepSeek) against manual extraction with WebPlotDigitizer on a complex solid-red-line glucose visualization. The paper explains the two main techniques LLMs use to read charts, fine-tuning and built-in reasoning architectures, and finds that current LLMs still fall short of manual methods for precise graph data extraction.

Read the paper ↗

Building a Resilient Career in the Age of AI

RESEARCH PAPER · RICE UNIVERSITY · DEC 2025

Argues that as generative and agentic AI automate white-collar cognitive tasks, uniquely human capabilities become the foundation of career resilience. Drawing on the EPOCH framework (Empathy, Presence, Opinion, Creativity, Hope), the paper maps the new job categories AI will create, trainers, explainers, and sustainers, and makes the case that education must shift from convergent, single-answer thinking toward the creative and ethical reasoning AI cannot replicate.

Read the paper ↗

Navigating Ethical Challenges Posed by AI

RESEARCH PAPER · WRITOPIA CONFERENCE FINALIST · MAY 2024

Framing AI as a "terrible beauty" that, like fire, is neither good nor bad, this essay examines three ethical dilemmas: biased training data (through the ProPublica recidivism algorithm case), AI's limited moral reasoning in trolley-problem scenarios like autonomous driving, and the lack of transparency and human control. Selected as a finalist for Writopia's Youth Essay Writing Conference.
